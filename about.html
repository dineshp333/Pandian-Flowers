About Pandian Flowers

Creating beautiful memories with traditional craftsmanship and fresh flowers for over 25 years

🌺

Our Story

Pandian Flowers was established in 2001 with a simple mission: to bring the beauty and fragrance of fresh flowers to every celebration. What started as a small family business has grown into one of the most trusted names in floral arrangements and garland making.

We are passionate about preserving traditional craftsmanship while embracing modern design aesthetics. Every garland we create is a work of art, carefully crafted by experienced artisans who understand the significance of flowers in Indian culture and celebrations.

Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for weddings, receptions, home decorations, and special events across the region.

## 📸 How to Upload Photos

- **Option B:** Go directly to `http://localhost:5500/public/admin.html`

### Step 2: Login
- Enter password: `flowerpandian1424`
- Click "Login"

### Step 3: Upload Photos
1. Select a category:
   - Wedding Garland
   - Reception Decoration
   - Home Nilavu
   - Custom Design

2. Choose a photo from your computer (JPG, PNG, GIF, etc.)
3. Click "Upload Photo"
4. ✅ Photo will be uploaded and displayed immediately!

### Step 4: Manage Photos

- Delete photos if needed
- Photos are stored in browser's local storage
